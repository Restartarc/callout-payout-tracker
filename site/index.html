How it works, and what it can't tell you

Where the numbers come from

pump.fun pays callout rewards as plain USDC transfers from one wallet, in a single batch each evening (around 19:00–21:00 UTC, a few thousand transfers in under ten minutes). We identified the wallet by matching a reward shown in the pump.fun app to its on-chain transfer, to the cent:

    Every payout on this site links to its transaction on Solscan, so you can check any number yourself. A payout's day is the UTC day its batch started.

    What this does not show

    • Whether payouts are fair. pump.fun says rewards depend on how much your followers profit, pay less for low-cap coins and cut back high-frequency callers, and the exact formula is private. Big differences in pay are expected under those rules, so unequal pay alone is not evidence of favouritism.
    • Which callouts earned a payout. The chain shows the transfer, not the reason. Perps and prediction-market callout rewards may come from the same wallet.
    • Who owns a wallet. Addresses are shown as they are. Nothing here says a wallet belongs to any person.
    • Payouts from other wallets. Only the payout wallet listed above is tracked. If pump.fun adds or changes wallets, totals will be incomplete until it is added.
